### The anti-bot check

To stop automatically created accounts, when an account is created and
at every sign-in the app does a small calculation in the background and
checks a field a person can't see. At account creation we also check
that the form wasn't submitted in under 4 seconds. At sign-in we note
whether the form was submitted in under one and a half seconds; this is
only a weak sign and never leads to a block on its own.

- The app keeps a **random code** on the phone (or in the browser),
  made once. It says nothing about you or the phone; we use it only to
  count new accounts created from the same device.
- **We don't keep the IP address in clear text** for this check, only an
  HMAC of it, computed with a secret key. This is pseudonymized data:
  without the key it doesn't lead back to the address, but we still treat
  it as personal data. The same goes for the app's code and the email
  address.
- An IP address or a device with suspicious attempts is **temporarily
  blocked** from creating new accounts: 24 hours, then 7 days. At most two
  accounts can be created from the same device in 24 hours. Signing in to
  an existing account isn't affected by these blocks.
- Only clear bot signs (the invisible field filled in, a forged or reused
  calculation) can lead to permanently banning an account. Weak signs (too
  fast, no calculation) are only noted. If it happened to you by mistake,
  write to us: the ban can be lifted.
- If the check refuses you, you see only a general message. The exact
  reason stays in the log, so we don't teach bots what to fix.

Legal basis: the legitimate interest of security, Art. 6(1)(f). The
signals are deleted after 90 days. When the account is deleted, the link
between the signals and the account disappears. The register of blocks
isn't deleted: a temporary block expires, and lifting a ban is added as
